Postdoc opportunities

Two 2-year postdocs in machine learning for 21cm cosmology (Paris, France)

We are hiring two postdocs to join the ANR project SLEEC (Self-supervised LEarning for Exploring 21cm Cosmology), which uses self-supervised and generative machine learning, in particular diffusion models, to get the most out of future SKA observations of the Epoch of Reionisation. The project builds on LoReLi, a public database of about 10,000 radiative-transfer simulations of reionisation.

  • Position 1 – LUX, Observatoire de Paris: learned summary statistics for 21cm inference (e.g. contrastive or self-supervised learning).
  • Position 2 – IAS, Université Paris-Saclay, Orsay: field-level inference with diffusion models, comparing DDPMs with Hamiltonian Monte Carlo sampling.

Profile: PhD in astrophysics, cosmology, physics, statistics or machine learning by the start date, with experience in machine learning (ideally generative or self-supervised models) and/or Bayesian inference. Experience with 21cm cosmology is a plus but not required.

Start date: between March and October 2027 (negotiable).
Deadline: 30 November 2026 · interviews in January 2027, selection in February 2027.

How to apply: upload a single PDF containing a CV with publication list, a research statement (max. 3 pages, excluding references), and a cover letter (including the names of two referees) at this link.
